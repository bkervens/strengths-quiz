Find Your Leadership Strengths Domain

12 quick questions. Pick the answer that is most like you. There are no right answers.

Strengths-based leaders start by knowing where they naturally excel. This snapshot sorts your answers into four domains of leadership strength: Executing, Influencing, Relationship Building, and Strategic Thinking. It takes about two minutes.

An informal class activity modeled on Gallup’s four domains. It is not the CliftonStrengths assessment.
